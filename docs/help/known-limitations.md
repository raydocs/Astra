# Known limitations

This page defines public-beta boundaries that should be visible before stronger product, platform, privacy, accessibility, billing, or launch claims are made.

## Pages and sites

- Some browser/internal pages cannot be translated.
- Some sites block extension access or dynamic content extraction.
- Site rules and Privacy Mode may intentionally reduce translation or memory behavior.

## Video and files

- Video learning depends on caption/transcript availability.
- Not every platform, caption type, fullscreen state, or SPA navigation path is guaranteed.
- PDF, EPUB, and subtitle-file flows have controlled proof paths, but universal document support is not claimed.

## Learning outcomes

- Astra can help learners read, understand, save, and review real content.
- Do not claim guaranteed fluency, guaranteed memory improvement, or broad learning outcomes without production cohort evidence.

## AI quality and safety

- Deterministic fixtures and safety tests exist.
- Production quality claims require a dated human-scored AI quality report and live provider evidence.

## Privacy and data deletion

- Metadata-only support bundles and local controls exist.
- Complete account/cloud/billing deletion claims require deployment and operational evidence for the target release.

## Billing and launch

- Current repo evidence supports free public-beta wording.
- Trial / Pro / paid checkout, refund, entitlement, store submission, and GTM launch-complete claims require external artifacts.

## Accessibility

- Accessibility foundations and browser-backed smoke evidence exist.
- Broad accessibility compliance claims require completed manual keyboard, contrast, scaled-text, and screen-reader evidence.
