# Why some pages cannot be translated

Some pages restrict what browser extensions can read or change. Astra may not translate pages when:

- the browser blocks extension access, such as browser settings, web store, or internal pages;
- the site uses strict security rules or cross-origin frames;
- the page loads content dynamically after Astra has already scanned it;
- the page has little readable text;
- the site is excluded by your own site rules or Privacy Mode choices.

What to try:

1. Refresh the page and retry.
2. Select a sentence and use a selection action instead of full-page translation.
3. Use Deep Read on a normal article page.
4. If reporting the issue, send a metadata-only support bundle. Hostname and feature surface are enough for first triage; page text and full URLs are not required by default.
