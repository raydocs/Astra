# Why a video has no captions

Astra can help with video learning when captions or transcripts are available to the extension. A video may have no usable captions when:

- the creator did not provide captions;
- automatic captions are unavailable or delayed;
- the platform blocks transcript access;
- the video is private, age-restricted, region-restricted, or otherwise unavailable;
- the page changed after Astra scanned it.

What to try:

1. Check whether the video platform itself shows captions.
2. Reload the video page.
3. Try another video with visible captions.
4. If you have a subtitle file, use the file/subtitle reader path when available.

Known limitation: do not claim universal video support. Caption availability depends on the source platform and video.
