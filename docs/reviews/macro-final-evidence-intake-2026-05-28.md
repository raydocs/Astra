## Operational rule

`pnpm check:macro-final-completion` validates that false fields keep evidenceLinks empty, that true fields have at least one link, that `evidenceLinks` include the matching machine-readable packet path/checklist path, that repo artifact paths exist in the worktree, and that placeholder or duplicate evidence links are rejected. It also validates manual QA checklist structure even before `manualQaChecklistComplete` is true: every required row must remain present exactly once as an operative checklist table row, not inside fenced examples or blockquotes, and untracked row, duplicate row, or non-not-run placeholder evidence link entries fail the gate. For operational evidence, CI artifacts, owner approval, manual QA rows, human-scored AI quality, billing/legal/store/GTM launch artifacts, and production metric exports, it parses the machine-readable packets/checklists and invokes the corresponding evaluator before allowing those blockers to clear. The checker does not prove external truth by itself; it prevents weak, ambiguous, duplicate, placeholder/sample, surrounding, embedded, or percent-encoded whitespace/control-character, or schema-less links from being used as final-completion evidence without rejecting valid packet paths merely because the path text omits descriptive evidence tokens.
